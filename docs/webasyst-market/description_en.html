Give bonus points an expiry date: unused points expire, and customers get a reminder in advance and come back to shop.

Some customers forget about the points they have collected, others save them “for later”. An expiry date and a reminder give them a reason to order now rather than someday.

Lifetime in days, counted from the date the points were credited
Weekly or monthly expiry: every Monday or on the 1st of the month
Any number of reminders by email and SMS, showing the amount about to expire
Fair calculation: only old points the customer never spent expire

The plugin's backend screens are available in English, Russian, Ukrainian, Belarusian, Kazakh, German, Dutch, French, Italian and Spanish. Default notification texts are provided in the same languages and are fully editable, and one notification can hold versions in several languages.

What the plugin does

Limits point lifetime

You set how many days the bonus points of the Shop-Script affiliate (bonus) program stay valid. Points not spent within that time expire.

Writes off expired points

Expired points are written off automatically on schedule: every Monday or on the 1st of every month. The write-off appears as a separate entry in the customer's bonus history.

Warns customers

Before the write-off, customers get an email or SMS saying how many points will expire and when. You choose the texts and the sending days.

Brings customers back

A reminder about points that are about to disappear is a good reason to visit the store and place an order with a discount.

When and which points expire

In the plugin settings you set the points' lifetime in days and the expiry schedule:

  • Weekly: points expire every Monday
  • Monthly: points expire on the 1st of every month
  • Never: the plugin neither writes off points nor sends reminders

On the expiry day, the plugin checks how many points the customer had exactly “lifetime” days ago. Everything the customer has spent since then is subtracted from that amount. What is left are old points that were never used, and those expire. Points credited later are not touched.

In other words, a customer always spends the oldest points first. If they order regularly and pay with points, nothing expires.

Example. Lifetime is 90 days, expiry is monthly. On March 1, the plugin looks at the balance as of December 1:

Date Event Balance
November 15 500 points credited for an order 500
January 10 300 points credited for a new order 800
February 5 200 points spent on an order 600
March 1 500 − 200 = 300 points expire: the unspent November points 300

The 300 points from January stay with the customer and expire only if they are not spent before their own expiry day.

Points do not expire on the very day their lifetime ends, but on the next expiry day after it. So in practice points live a bit longer than the set lifetime: up to a week longer with weekly expiry and up to a month longer with monthly expiry. A round number works well, for example 365 days: “points are valid for a year”.

Expired points in the customer's bonus history: old points are written off, recently credited points stay on the balance

A soft start for points already collected

If your bonus program has been running for a while, many customers already have points older than the chosen lifetime. To keep them from expiring without warning on the very first run, you can postpone the start of expiry to a date of your choice.

Points do not expire before that date. Send reminders and give customers time to spend what they have collected.

Settings: point lifetime, expiry schedule and postponed start

Expiry reminders

You can create as many notifications as you need, each one an email or SMS with its own text and schedule. For each notification you choose the sending day:

  • A day of the month, for example the 25th. If you choose the 29th, 30th or 31st, then in months without that day the notification is sent on the last day of the month
  • A day of the week, for example Thursday

You can also set a time: the notification is then sent on the chosen day no earlier than that hour. Each notification is sent once on its day.

Only customers whose points will expire on the next expiry day receive it. The text can show how many points will expire, the customer's name, the current balance, the points' lifetime and the next expiry date. Texts support HTML and Smarty; a new notification starts from a ready-made email or SMS template.

If your customers speak different languages, add language versions to a notification, each with its own subject and text. A customer gets the version in the language set in their profile, or the default text if there is no such version. Each customer receives one message, and all versions share the same schedule.

Before turning a notification on, you can test it: pick a customer whose points are about to expire and send a test message to any address. The test dialog shows how many points the customer has now, how many will expire and how many will remain.

Reminder strategies. With monthly expiry, two emails work well: a week before the month ends (say, on the 22nd) and on the last day of the month (the 31st). With weekly expiry, one reminder on Thursday or Friday gives the customer the weekend to order. The amount in a reminder is calculated when it is sent: if the customer spends some points, the next reminder shows a smaller amount.

Getting started

  1. In your hosting control panel, schedule the plugin's command with cron, preferably once an hour. The exact command is shown in the plugin settings
  2. In Marketing → Affiliate program → the plugin's tab, set the points' lifetime and the expiry schedule
  3. If customers already have old points, postpone the start of expiry to give them time
  4. In Marketing → expiry notifications, create reminders and check them with a test message

Write-offs and reminders are run by a script on a schedule. If the script does not run even once on an expiry day or on a notification's sending day, that write-off or mailing is skipped. That is why we recommend running it once an hour rather than once a day.
